Section 1 of 4

App data & platform access.

These give me visibility into almost™'s real numbers and how the app is currently performing. Required before Milestone 1 research begins.

Required to begin Helpful if it exists
Required
App download & install data
Total downloads, installs, active users, and any retention or drop-off data you have. Export from the App Store Connect or Google Play Console if available.
Required
Waitlist data
Total waitlist size, when it was collected, and any demographic or geographic information you have on signups. Even a raw export is fine.
Required
In-app behavior data
How users move through the app once they're in: completed profiles, trips added, connections made, and any measure of when two users cross paths. If your app tracks analytics through a tool your developer set up (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Firebase, or similar), an export from there works. If not, whatever numbers you can pull into a simple spreadsheet is fine.
Helpful
Any user feedback or reviews
App store reviews, DMs, emails, beta tester feedback, anything users have said about the experience so far.
Helpful
Access to analytics dashboards (view only)
A view-only login, shared link, or even screenshots of App Store Connect, Google Play Console, Mixpanel, or equivalent. Not required if you can export or share the numbers directly.

Section 4 of 4

Access to real users.

Milestone 1 includes customer research with current or prospective almost™ users. I'll design and conduct this, but I need your help getting access to the right people.

Required
Intro to 3–5 current users or beta testers
People who have downloaded and used the app, even briefly. A warm introduction by email or DM is the fastest path. I'll handle the outreach and conversation from there.
Required
Access to 3–5 waitlist contacts
People who signed up but haven't used the app yet. Understanding why they signed up and what they're expecting is as valuable as current user feedback. A warm intro or permission to reach out directly.
Helpful
Any existing user surveys or interviews
If you've already gathered feedback through a survey, interview, or conversation, share it. Even informal Slack or DM conversations with users count.
