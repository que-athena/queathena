The Real Challenge

The problem isn't awareness. It's density.

almost™'s promise is beautiful and specific: your next connection, platonic, romantic, or business, could be the person in the lounge right now. But that promise only comes alive when enough of the right people are in the same place at the same time.

That means the goal isn't installs everywhere. It's concentrated installs, in the right hub, with the right travelers, so the app delivers on its promise from day one and those first users bring the next ones.

This sprint is engineered around that. A growth strategy built to launch almost™ in Nashville with enough density to work, then replicate the playbook to the next city. Every part maps to one goal: installs and signups.
